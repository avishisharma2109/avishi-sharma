PlaceMe AI

Turns college placement emails into a searchable company directory, straight from your Gmail inbox.

  • Gmail OAuth 2.0 feeds a LangChain + Groq chain with a PydanticOutputParser that pulls 14 structured fields per email across 11 REST endpoints.
  • Sync built to survive free-tier timeouts: IDs de-duplicated before fetching, batches of 8 committed per email so no work is lost.
  • 2-provider fallback (Groq → Gemini, 2s/email) with regex heuristics when the models fail.
  • Runs at $0/month on Vercel, Render and Supabase with JWT auth, Fernet-encrypted OAuth tokens, rate limiting and 20 CI tests.
  • React
  • FastAPI
  • LangChain
  • Groq
  • Gemini
  • PostgreSQL
  • OAuth 2.0
